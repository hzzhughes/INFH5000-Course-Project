'Is this seat free?' asks a boy. It is the first day of term. 'Yes, you can sit here,' says Ana. The new boy puts his bag on the floor. 'My name is Peter,' he says. 'My name is Ana,' she says. 'I have a new book about trains.' 'Can I see it?' asks Peter. 'Yes. You must be quiet in the reading room,' says Ana. 'I will be,' says Peter. While the teacher writes on the board, the two children look at the book together. They do not say a word.
