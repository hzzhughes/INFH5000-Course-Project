A bicycle is a machine with many parts, and each part has a job. The frame holds the rider and the other parts together, but it does not move by itself. The two wheels roll on the ground. The chain joins the pedals to the back wheel, so when the rider pushes the pedals, the wheel turns. The brakes slow the bike down when the rider squeezes the handles. Although the parts are simple, they must work together, because a bike with a loose chain will not move. A good rider checks the tyres and the chain before every ride.
