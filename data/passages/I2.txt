People make bread from flour, and flour comes from wheat. A farmer cuts the wheat in a field. He takes it to a machine. The machine turns the wheat into flour. A baker mixes the flour with water, salt and yeast. Yeast is a small living thing. It eats the sugar in the flour and makes gas. The gas makes holes in the dough. After the baker shapes the dough, the hot oven bakes it. The bread comes out brown, and it smells good. The baker sells it in the morning.
