Tom was playing in the yard, and the sky became dark. His mother called him in because the rain was coming. He ran to the door and put his boots on. His boots were left by the door. While he was looking for his raincoat, the water came in through the open door. The raincoat was still on the chair in the hall. It was wet, so Tom asked his sister for a towel. His sister was reading a book. She gave him the towel and closed the window. Then they sat down and looked at the rain. The rain was loud on the roof.
