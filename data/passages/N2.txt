A girl called Ana is on a bus. She has a blue box in her hands. The box is for her new shoes. The bus is late because of the rain. Ana looks at her bag. The shoes are not in the bag. The box is empty. She is sad. Then she looks under the seat. The shoes are under the seat. Ana takes them out. She puts them in the box. She smiles at the window. The rain stops and the sun comes out. She is late for school today.
