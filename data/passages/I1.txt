A seed is a part of a plant. It has a hard coat. The coat keeps the seed safe. A seed needs three things. It needs water. It needs air. It needs warm soil. Then the seed begins to grow. A small root goes down into the soil. The plant grows up to the sun. The plant will grow bigger every day. Some seeds sleep for many months. They wait for the cold to pass. Then the warm rain wakes them up. Every seed has a small plant inside it. That plant is waiting to grow.
