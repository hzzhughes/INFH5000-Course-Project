Ben wanted to ride his new bike to the park, but the chain made a loud noise. He got off and looked at the chain. The chain was loose, so he asked his father for help. His father showed him how to fix it. Although the repair took a long time, Ben did not want to give up. If he fixed the chain, he could go to the park before it got dark. The noise stopped, and Ben rode home very fast. He put the tools back in the box and left the box in the shed. The sun was low over the trees, and the sky was orange.
