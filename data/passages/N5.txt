The school garden was empty for two years, because the old trees had fallen and nobody planted anything new. A new class asked their teacher whether they could grow food there, although several children thought it would be too hard. The teacher said that if they worked together, they could start with beans and tomatoes, since these plants do not need much water. So they cleared the ground, and although the work was tiring, they finished it in three weeks. Because they took turns every day, the garden stayed alive. By the end of the term, they had picked more vegetables than the school could eat, so they gave half of them to the families.
