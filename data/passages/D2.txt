'Where are my shoes?' asks Mei. She looks under the bed. 'They are not here,' she says. 'They were here, because I put them by the door this morning.' Her brother comes into the room. 'Maybe the dog took them,' he says. Mei looks at the dog. The dog looks at the floor. 'Look,' says Mei. 'The dog has one shoe in his mouth.' 'Now we know where the shoes are!' Then she takes the shoe away and puts the shoes on. Mei puts her shoes on and goes out.
