'We will miss the last train,' says Rui. The rain is heavier now, and the station is far away. 'If we run, we get wet, but if we wait, we miss it,' says his sister. 'Then we should ask the driver,' says Rui. They wave down a bus, although the driver is in a hurry. 'The station is closed,' he says, 'but I can take you to the next one.' At the next station, because the train is late, they have time for hot tea. When the train comes, Rui says they would be wet and cold if they had run. They are warm, and the train arrives as they sit down.
