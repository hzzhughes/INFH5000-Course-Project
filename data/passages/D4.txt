'I do not want to go in,' says Tom. He stands by the side of the pool. 'The water is cold, because it is morning,' says the coach. 'But the sun is coming out,' says Tom. 'If you put your foot in, it will feel better,' says the coach. Tom puts one foot in the cold water. 'That was not so bad,' he says. Although he still did not want to jump, he counts to three and jumps in. The water is cold, but soon his body feels warm. 'That was the best thing I did today,' he tells his mother that evening.
