Rain comes from the sea. The sea gets its water from rivers and from the ground. The sun is warm, so it pulls the water up into the air. This water is called water vapour. While the vapour rises, it becomes cold, and the cold water turns back into tiny drops. Millions of these drops together make a cloud. The cloud is heavy, so the drops fall down as rain. When the rain reaches the ground, it runs into the rivers again. The whole journey starts once more. The water is always on the move.
