A girl has a bag. Her friend comes to play. 'Hello,' says the girl. 'What is in your bag?' asks the friend. 'I do not know,' says the girl. Then she looks in the bag. 'It is a cat!' she says. The cat says nothing. 'Is it your cat?' asks the friend. 'Yes, it is,' says the girl. Then they play with the cat. 'Look at the cat,' says the girl. 'It is black and white.' 'My father gave it to me last week.' 'He said I must feed it every day.' 'Now I am afraid it will run away.' 'Come in,' says the girl. 'You can hold it. Be careful.'
